About DivyaSeva

A simple devotion-tech startup for Indian families.

DivyaSeva is designed as a clean platform where users can book puja, daan and sankalp services without confusion, heavy text or boring design.

Our approach

We keep the UI simple, familiar and trustworthy for Indian users. The website avoids unnecessary clutter and focuses on a direct path: choose seva, fill details and track booking.

Startup-ready

The code is split into multiple pages, common CSS and reusable JavaScript. Firebase files are included so you can connect authentication and Firestore when you launch.
